What it is the setup

Hulaan Mo! drops you in the middle of a barangay fiesta, where a secret number between 1 and 100 stands between you and a plate of your favorite Filipino food. You get ten guesses — each one nudges you closer or further, "mataas" or "mababa," until you land the number and claim your premyo.

Where it comes from the concept

The game borrows its energy from real barangay fiestas — the kind built on bayanihan, good food, and a crowd cheering someone on at a game booth. Tagalog prompts, food-based prizes, and fiesta color throughout keep that spirit intact rather than treating it as a skin over a generic quiz.

  • Tagalog prompts and hints
  • Philippine flag palette
  • Food-based premyo
  • Barangay fiesta setting
  • Filipino proverbs as hints
